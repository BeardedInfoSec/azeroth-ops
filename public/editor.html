SHORTCUTS & HELP
Placing
Pick a tile to arm it; every click places a copy. Esc, right-click or DONE exits. Shift-click always keeps placing.
R / Shift+R rotate by the angle snap · [ and ] or Alt+wheel scale · the next copy keeps your rotation and scale.
Selecting
Click selects · Shift-click adds · drag a box in PLAN view · Ctrl+A all · Alt-drag duplicates and moves · G toggles gizmo space.
Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V (at cursor) / Ctrl+D · arrows nudge by the grid (Shift x5) · PageUp/PageDown height · R and [ ] work on a selection too · Delete removes · F frames the selection.
Camera
WASD + Q/E move · right-drag look or pan · wheel zoom · V plan/fly · 1 2 3 gizmo tool.
Other
P spawn-point tool (click adds, drag moves, R rotates, Delete removes) · T scatter tool · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo (each click, paste, scatter or group move is one step). Layouts hold up to 256 added assets.

SPAWN POINTS

Click the ground to add a spawn. Click a marker to select it; drag it to move. R / Shift+R rotates the selected spawn (or the next one). Delete removes. Cyan markers are the automatic spawns, so gaps show where to add your own.

New assets drop onto the surface under the cursor plus this offset. A red ghost means it overlaps solid geometry (best effort). Always check placement in game after saving.
